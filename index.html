How SmartTriage Works

👤

Step 1: Basic Information

Share your personal details and medical history to help doctors understand your background quickly.

🩺

Step 2: Symptom Assessment

Describe your symptoms in detail. Our AI analyzes the information to determine urgency level.

⚕️

Step 3: Triage Results

Get your urgency assessment (Low, Moderate, or Emergency) with personalized care recommendations.

📍

Step 4: Find Care & Download

Locate nearby facilities and download a professional PDF summary to bring to your appointment.

Emergency Warning

If experiencing severe symptoms like chest pain, difficulty breathing, severe bleeding, or loss of consciousness, call 108 (Emergency) or 102 (Ambulance) immediately. Do not rely on this app for emergencies.
